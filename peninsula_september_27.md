## Coverage gap: Furnished Finder is rate-limiting

Furnished Finder began returning Cloudflare "Attention Required" pages partway through this work.
A whole-unit sweep of Richmond, San Pablo, El Cerrito, Pinole, Hercules and Vallejo returned **zero
results because every request was blocked**, not because those cities are empty. The same applies to
the earlier attempts at Daly City, South San Francisco, Emeryville, Sausalito and a fresh Palo Alto
pass. These remain genuinely unchecked and are worth retrying once the limit clears. SUpost and
Nominatim/OSRM were unaffected, and all findings above come from SUpost.

## Standing conclusion

Across the East Bay, the Peninsula, San Francisco and the Solano corridor, **no listing on an approved
platform meets all four constraints at once except El Sobrante** ($1,475, one night late). The binding
pair is private bathroom + $1,500: relaxing either one opens real inventory immediately, and relaxing
neither leaves the current eleven cards.
