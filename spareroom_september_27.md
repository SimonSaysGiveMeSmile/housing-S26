# SpareRoom sweep — September 27, 2026

Prompted by a hard constraint stated this evening: **month-by-month lease or sublet only.** That rule
disqualifies most of what the other platforms carry, so the search moved to SpareRoom, which had never
been swept. It lists **Minimum term** and **Maximum term** as structured fields, which makes it the
only source that can be filtered on exactly this requirement.

Searched: San Mateo, Burlingame and Millbrae with the `?parking=Y` filter, plus every ad those pages
surfaced across neighbouring San Mateo County districts. 14 unique ads with parking; each promising one
was opened individually. SpareRoom was **not** rate-limited, unlike Furnished Finder.
