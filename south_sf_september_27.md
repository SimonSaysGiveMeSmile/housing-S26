# South San Francisco sweep — September 27, 2026

Brief: find more rooms like the Millbrae one in southern San Francisco under $1,500, ideally
**$1,000–$1,200**. Furnished or part-furnished is fine for a short stay. Report whether parking is
assigned, and the dates, deposit and house rules.

"Southern San Francisco" was read both ways, and both were covered: the **city of South San
Francisco** and its neighbours, and the **southern neighbourhoods of San Francisco proper**.

searching neighbouring towns, not from paging.

## The two worth waiting for — now on the board as `waitlist`

**South San Francisco, Sunshine Gardens — $1,050** (ad 103156861). Private bathroom. Parking
advertised. **Furnished.** Utilities included. **Deposit $500**, the lowest seen anywhere in this
search. **Minimum term None, maximum term None** — the same month-to-month terms as Millbrae, at $450
less. Available now. Shared four-bedroom house; the three existing roommates are long-term civil
servants with background clearances who have rented it over four years. No shared living room. The
advertiser wants an established professional with a clean background. 12 mi to SF, 24 to Stanford.

**Daly City, St. Francis — $1,100** (ad 103169912). Private bathroom, **assigned parking space**,
patio onto the back yard, shared kitchen, on-site laundry. Deposit $1,600. 11 mi to SF, 29 to
Stanford. Two cautions: the ad's own text has its numbers stripped out, so its stated lease length is
unreadable; and screening is heavy — full application per adult, photo ID, two months of pay stubs, a
minimum credit score, no prior evictions, and a *positive, not neutral* reference from the most recent
landlord.

Together these are the cheapest private-bath rooms found anywhere in this search, and the cheapest
private-bath-plus-assigned-parking room is the $1,100. They cost $400–$450 less than Millbrae for the
same terms.

## Open, but each fails a constraint

- **Oceanview, $1,150** — the closest miss. Genuinely **no minimum and no maximum term**, available
  now, deposit $1,150, only one other person in a two-bedroom house, yard, wifi. But the bathroom is
  **shared**, **parking is "No"**, and it is **unfurnished**. Housemate prefers female.
- **West Portal, $1,290** — parking, furnished, utilities included, 1–3 month term with short rentals
  considered. But the $1,290 room **shares one of three bathrooms**; only the $2,000 room has a
  private bath. The house rules are unusually strict and worth reading before contacting: COVID
  vaccine mandatory, mask for check-in, **no visitors, family or friends, ever, under any
  circumstances**, bedrooms cannot be locked when you leave, silence between 8pm and 10am, and a
  request to "take one shower a day as short as possible".
- **Bayshore, $1,200** — **6-month minimum**, so it fails the lease rule outright. Also parking "No"
  with street parking only, and the ad contradicts itself: SpareRoom's structured field says private
  bathroom while the description says "shared bathroom on the main level". $2,200 to move in.

## Flagged: two ads that should not be contacted

The **$950 Daly City** and **$1,380 Serramonte** ads are the only other "free to contact" listings
under $1,500. Both are syndicated through **TurboTenant** by a single advertiser whose other
properties span Wildomar, Susanville, Los Feliz, El Cajon, Visalia, Sacramento, Long Beach, Inglewood
and Oceanside. Both have prices stripped out of the description ("room is $ per month"), both offer
only shared bathrooms, and the $950 pushes contact to a personal WhatsApp handle. SpareRoom itself
notes these "have not been fully moderated". Treated as lead generation rather than real local rooms,
and excluded.

## What this changes

The earlier structural finding was that private bathroom + $1,500 was the binding pair. South San
Francisco refines it: **at $1,050–$1,100 this corridor really does offer private bath, parking,
furniture and month-to-month terms** — the constraint is not price or geography but *timing*. Every
room at that price is already spoken for, and several turned over within the last week.

That makes two actions worth more than further searching: contact Millbrae now, since it is the only
full match anyone can actually reply to; and re-check the two watch-list ads daily, because a pending
lease that falls through would beat it by $400.
