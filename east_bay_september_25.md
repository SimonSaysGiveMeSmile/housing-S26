## Method note

City-to-SF distances for Berkeley (12.2 mi), Alameda (14.1 mi) and El Sobrante (19.5 mi) were added to
`distance_estimates.json` the same way as the existing ones: Nominatim geocoding and an OSRM driving
route, one request at a time with an identifying user agent and at least 1.1 seconds between calls, per
the [Nominatim usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/). They are
city-center road distances, not property-to-door mileage and not live traffic.
